# 💰 JeevanFlow 

### AI-Powered Financial Journey & Decision-Support Platform

JeevanFlow AI is an intelligent financial decision-support platform that helps users understand their financial health, analyze major financial goals, identify funding gaps, and explore the potential impact of financial decisions.

The platform combines **Machine Learning, Explainable AI, Financial Analytics, and What-If Simulation** into a single application.

---

## 🌟 Why JeevanFlow ?

Major financial decisions such as weddings, education, buying a house, unexpected expenses, or dealing with job loss can significantly affect a person's financial stability.

JeevanFlow helps users answer questions such as:

- How financially healthy am I?
- Can I realistically plan this financial goal?
- How large is my funding gap?
- How much disposable income do I have?
- How much of my income is committed to EMIs?
- How long could my savings support my current expenses?
- What factors are influencing my financial assessment?
- What happens if my financial situation changes?

Instead of providing a single financial recommendation, JeevanFlow focuses on **transparent analysis and explainable insights**.

---

# 🚀 Key Features

## 🧠 AI Financial Journey Detection

Users can describe their financial situation in natural language.

Example:

> "I need 4 lakh for my sister's wedding."

The Machine Learning service identifies the relevant financial journey.

Supported journeys include:

- 💍 WEDDING
- 🎓 EDUCATION
- 🏠 HOUSE
- 🏥 MEDICAL
- 💼 JOB LOSS
- 💸 EXPENSE

The system also provides a model confidence score and handles uncertain inputs.

---

## 📊 Financial Health Analysis

JeevanFlow analyzes a user's financial profile using:

- Monthly income
- Monthly expenses
- Existing EMI obligations
- Savings
- Insurance coverage

The system calculates financial indicators such as:

- Financial health score
- Risk level
- Disposable income
- Debt-to-income ratio
- Emergency runway

---

## 🔍 Explainable AI

JeevanFlow doesn't simply display a financial assessment.

It explains **why** the assessment was produced.

The Explainable AI module provides:

- Key financial factors
- Current risk level
- Debt-to-income ratio
- Emergency runway
- Analysis assumptions

Example factors may include:

- EMI burden
- Emergency savings buffer
- Disposable income after expenses and EMI

This makes the analysis easier to understand and audit.

---
